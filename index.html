Welcome

Light, fresh, and easy to enjoy

Visitors describe a great pub interior that is light and fresh, a good selection of drinks at the bar, and a limited menu that covers all the expected pub staples — cooked freshly and served with care.

The chips get a special mention: crisp and crunchy outside, light and fluffy inside. Prices are good for nice food, and the team is pleased to see you, helpful and attentive without being over the top.

Two beer gardens & a function room

Inside, outside, or all to yourselves

Drink inside or in one of the two beer gardens, or book the Secret Garden function room — with its own garden — for a gathering of up to around 40 people, depending on your requirements.

Buffets are available for a variety of events, and the team will help you plan the room, the food and the layout. For booking or enquiries, call 01482 847113 or email bluebellhu16@gmail.com.

Food, Thursday to Sunday

The food menu is served Thursday evening to Sunday tea time. These are the dishes and nights visitors name most.

Thursday Steak Night

Gatherings & buffets

For the days that matter

Birthdays, celebrations, festive meals and family gatherings all find a home here — including buffets for groups, with food praised for variety, value, quality and taste.

Visitors have thanked the team for looking after difficult occasions with a friendly, caring approach and service where nothing was too much trouble. The festive menu — turkey with roast potatoes, Christmas pudding and a cheese board — has earned full marks from Christmas diners.

When is food served?

Food is served Thursday evening to Sunday tea time. Drinks are available whenever the pub is open.

Can I book the function room?

Yes — the Secret Garden room with its own garden suits gatherings of up to around 40 people. Buffets are available for a variety of events.

Is there a steak night?

Yes — Thursday is steak night at The Blue Bell, and visitors praise the steak meals.

Do you cater for large groups?

Yes — buffets for groups are available, and the function room can be booked for gatherings. Call 01482 847113 to discuss your requirements.
